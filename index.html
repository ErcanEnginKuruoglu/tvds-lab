What our students are building.

Research led by the students of the group — from heavy-tailed statistics to brain network learning. Each project below is theirs to drive.

Heavy-Tailed Statistics α-Stable Learning & Non-Gaussian Processes Bo Shu · Jipeng Li α-stable and non-Gaussian models for heavy-tailed, impulsive data — from robust Bayesian inference to the structure of neural-network weights. Read more → Representation Learning Structured & Efficient Representation Learning Chengfeng Wu Representations that separate task-relevant semantics from nuisance, with efficient temporal aggregation on dynamic graphs. Read more → Brain Network Learning LOSC — Learning Optimal Spectral Clustering Functional brain networks A graph-theory-grounded framework that exploits the small-world topology of functional brain networks for more accurate, interpretable classification.
