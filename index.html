Learning from data that changes over time.

The Time-Varying Data Science Group is a team of students and researchers, led by Prof. Ercan E. Kuruoglu, building statistical, Bayesian, and graph-based methods for data that evolves — across time and over networks.

Student projects

What our students are building.

The research is led by the students of the group. Here are four of their current projects.

Heavy-Tailed Statistics α-Stable Learning & Non-Gaussian Processes Bo Shu · Jipeng Li α-stable and non-Gaussian models for heavy-tailed, impulsive data — from robust Bayesian inference to the structure of neural-network weights. Read more → Representation Learning Structured & Efficient Representation Learning Chengfeng Wu Representations that separate task-relevant semantics from nuisance, with efficient temporal aggregation on dynamic graphs. Read more → Brain Network Learning LOSC — Learning Optimal Spectral Clustering Functional brain networks A graph-theory-grounded framework that exploits the small-world topology of functional brain networks for more accurate, interpretable classification. Read more → Brain Network Learning HB-GIB — Hierarchical Brain Graph Information Bottleneck Functional brain networks A community-level information bottleneck that preserves biologically meaningful brain structure while capturing cross-community interactions.
